# ADR-0087: SCIM 2.0 provisioning of a company's members for Enterprise, with a company token the OWNER issues, deactivation as suspension, and the seat fallback to VIEWER

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-22
- Decided by: the owner
- Sources: [PLATFORM.md](../PLATFORM.md) §3, §4, §9 (governance); [CONVENTIONS.md](../CONVENTIONS.md) §4, §5 (bearer credentials, personal access tokens, a provider's company), §6 (authorization matrix, seats, `LAST_OWNER`), §8 (audit), §15.11 (erasure), §15.13 (suspension, a member who leaves), §15.24 (the licence, the seat check, `SEAT_FALLBACK`, `ee/`); [ADR-0045](0045-mcp-server-for-peoples-clients.md) (personal access tokens: format and hashing); [ADR-0058](0058-authority-from-the-audience.md) (the roles); [ADR-0075](0075-companies-and-sign-in-from-the-deployment.md) (companies and providers declared in the deployment); [ADR-0077](0077-licence-editions-and-seats.md) (`Feature.SCIM`, seats, expiry); [ADR-0086](0086-saml-sign-in.md) (SAML sign-in, `_AUTO_PROVISION`, `_SCIM_MATCH`); [ADR-0088](0088-roles-from-the-identity-provider.md) (the role from the provider, which SCIM's role follows); RFC 7642, RFC 7643 and RFC 7644 (SCIM 2.0); Microsoft's "Develop and plan provisioning for a SCIM endpoint" and Okta's "Build a SCIM API service"; the [threat model](../threat-model.md), TB3, 3.6, R8, §6.1

## Context

- A company's people come and go at its identity provider. Today a new person gets a user at their first OIDC sign-in, with the provider's role (ADR-0075), and a person who leaves keeps their Tinfirm account and their sessions until an admin removes them (threat model 3.6, R8): nothing tells Tinfirm.
- SCIM 2.0 is how Entra ID and Okta push users to an application: they create, update and deactivate users through a REST API the application serves, authenticated by a bearer token their admin pastes in. SCIM is an Enterprise feature (ADR-0077, decision 4).
- Tinfirm's model differs from a plain user list in three ways that SCIM must respect. A user is the instance's and may be a member of several companies, each with its own role. A seat belongs to a role, checked when a role is given (`409 SEATS_EXHAUSTED`), while a provider's new user falls back to VIEWER (ADR-0077 decision 5). And a person's content is kept when they leave; erasing it is a separate act of the OWNER (CONVENTIONS §15.11).
- Sign-in identifies a user by `(issuer, subject)` and never links by email (CONVENTIONS §5). A user SCIM creates has no sign-in yet, so SCIM and sign-in must agree on who is who.

## Decision

**Each company that the deployment allows serves SCIM 2.0 `Users` at `/scim/v2`, under an Enterprise key, to a bearer token its OWNER issues. SCIM creates and updates the company's members, deactivates by suspending them at once, and removes them as a member who leaves, never erasing their content. A role that holds a seat falls back to VIEWER when none is free. A user SCIM creates is bound to their first sign-in by the IdP's `externalId`.**

1. **The endpoint.** `{TINFIRM_PUBLIC_URL}/scim/v2`, one base URL for the instance; the token names the company (decision 3). It serves:

   | Path | Methods |
   |---|---|
   | `/scim/v2/Users` | `GET` (list, `filter`, `startIndex`, `count`), `POST` |
   | `/scim/v2/Users/{id}` | `GET`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` |
   | `/scim/v2/ServiceProviderConfig`, `/ResourceTypes`, `/Schemas` | `GET` |
   | `/scim/v2/ResourceTypes/{name}`, `/Schemas/{id}` | `GET`: each at the `meta.location` the lists give it (RFC 7644 §4) |

   Media type `application/scim+json` (and `application/json`, which Okta sends); errors in SCIM's own form (RFC 7644 §3.12: `schemas`, `status`, `scimType`, `detail`), never `problem+json`. `ServiceProviderConfig` says: `patch` yes; `filter` yes, at most 100 results; `bulk`, `changePassword`, `sort` and `etag` no; authentication `oauthbearertoken`. **No `/Groups`** (decision 5), no `/Bulk`, no `/Me`; they answer `404`.
   - **Filters**: `userName eq "…"`, `externalId eq "…"` and `id eq "…"`, case-insensitive for `userName` (RFC 7643: `caseExact` false); any other filter is `400 invalidFilter`. Pages of at most 100 (`count`), 1-based `startIndex`, ordered by the user's id over the index `(company_id, user_id)` of the company's members. SCIM's lists are index-based (RFC 7644 §3.4.2.4), so this is the one place that reads with an offset, an exception to CONVENTIONS §7's keyset rule that §7 names: the offset is bounded, `startIndex` above 10 000 is `400 invalidValue`, and the scan stays inside one company's rows. A filter (`userName eq`, `externalId eq`), which is how Entra ID and Okta match people, is an index lookup and needs no page; only Okta's import lists every member.
   - **Scope**: the token's company only. The users it sees are the company's members, every role, suspended ones included; a user who is not a member is `404` to it, as an unknown id is.
   - **A stateless chain of its own**, as the MCP endpoint's: no session, no CSRF, the session cookie ignored, a request that carries an `Origin` header refused `403`. A company's requests are limited (`tinfirm.scim.requests-per-minute`, default 600 per company), and refused tokens per client address (20 a minute), both `429` with `Retry-After`, which Entra ID and Okta honour.

2. **Allowed per company by the deployment.** `TINFIRM_COMPANIES_<KEY>_SCIM_ENABLED` (Helm `companies[].scim.enabled`), `false` by default, and `_SCIM_ROLE`, the role of a new user whose request names none, `MEMBER` by default (`ADMIN`, `MEMBER` or `VIEWER`; never `OWNER`), and `_SCIM_ROLE_SOURCE`, `IDP` by default or `APP`, who decides the role after creation ([ADR-0088](0088-roles-from-the-identity-provider.md) decision 8). As ADR-0075 leaves who may sign in to the operator, so SCIM, which creates users of the instance, needs the operator's yes for each company. The company's OWNER then issues the token in the app (decision 3).

3. **The company's token.** Issued, rotated and revoked in the app by the company's OWNER, because it is a secret the IdP's admin pastes and must be replaceable without a restart, and because the OWNER answers for who joins the company.
   - **Issued** with `POST /api/companies/{companyId}/scim/tokens {name, expiresAt?}` (a new permission, `MANAGE_PROVISIONING`, OWNER only; the licence holds `SCIM`; `409 SCIM_NOT_ENABLED` when the deployment has not allowed it): `tfs_` and 43 base64url characters (32 random bytes), shown once in the answer and never again.
   - **Stored hashed**: `scim_token` holds the company, a name, `hex(sha256(token))` (unique), who created it and when, an optional expiry, `last_used_at` (at most a minute stale) and the revocation; never the token. A token is resolved by its hash before any tenant is known, as a personal access token is (CONVENTIONS §5), then the request runs in its company's tenant context.
   - **Rotated** by overlap: a company holds at most two active tokens (`409 SCIM_TOKEN_LIMIT`), so the OWNER issues the next, pastes it at the IdP, and revokes the old one (`DELETE …/scim/tokens/{tokenId}`, OWNER, in every edition, so that a token can be stopped after the licence ended too).
   - **Refused**: a missing, unknown, malformed, revoked or expired token, or one whose company's SCIM the deployment no longer allows, is `401` with `WWW-Authenticate: Bearer error="invalid_token"`, without saying which.
   - **Audited**: `scim.token.created` and `scim.token.revoked` in the company's chain, with the token's id, name and expiry, by the OWNER; its use shows in `last_used_at` and in the actions it causes (decision 9). A refused token is audited in the instance chain (`scim.refused`, reason and client address), at most once a minute per address and reason.
   - **The actor** of everything a token does is the token (`Actor` kind `SCIM`, its id), so that the audit names which token, of whom, changed a member.

4. **The attributes.** The core `User` schema (`urn:ietf:params:scim:schemas:core:2.0:User`) and one extension of Tinfirm's:

   | Attribute | Tinfirm | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | `id` | the user's id | Tinfirm's, read-only |
   | `userName` | the user's email | required, unique on the instance, normalised to lower case; Entra ID sends the UPN, which the guide keeps equal to the mail |
   | `externalId` | the membership's `scim_external_id` | required on `POST` (absent or empty: `400 invalidValue`), since a sign-in binds by it; the IdP's stable id of the person, unique in the company; binds the first sign-in (decision 6) |
   | `emails` | the user's email | the `primary` or `work` value; when it differs from `userName`, `userName` wins and `emails` is answered as stored |
   | `name.givenName`, `name.familyName`, `name.formatted`, `displayName` | the user's name | `displayName`, else `name.formatted`, else given and family names joined; at most 100 characters |
   | `active` | the membership is in effect: the member is not suspended, by SCIM or an admin, and the user is active (decision 7) | `true` or `false`; `false` suspends; `true` lifts only what SCIM did, so an admin's suspension or deactivation stays and the answer stays `false`; the strings `"True"` and `"False"` that Entra ID sends are accepted |
   | `urn:ietf:params:scim:schemas:extension:tinfirm:2.0:User:role` | the member's role | `admin`, `member`, `viewer` (any case); `owner` is answered for an OWNER and refused in a request (`400 invalidValue`). Under `_SCIM_ROLE_SOURCE=IDP` SCIM owns the role: it applies whenever a request carries it, and the app cannot change it (`409 ROLE_MANAGED`, "managed by SCIM"); under `APP` it applies at creation only (ADR-0088 decision 8) |
   | `meta` | `resourceType`, `created`, `lastModified`, `location` | read-only |

   Every other attribute (`password`, `phoneNumbers`, `addresses`, `title`, the enterprise extension, `groups`) is accepted and ignored, never stored; `password` in particular. A `PATCH` takes `add`, `replace` and `remove` in any case, with or without a `path`, and paths of the attributes above, `emails[type eq "work"].value` included; a `remove` of `userName` or `externalId`, which are required and bind the sign-in, is `400 invalidValue`, and a `replace` of either with an empty value too. A `PUT` replaces the user as RFC 7644 §3.5.1 says: the name and `displayName` it omits are cleared (the name falls back to the email's local part), `active` omitted is `true`, `userName` and `externalId` are required; the role is the one exception, replaced only when the extension is present, so that Okta's `PUT` of a whole user without a role mapping never resets it (ADR-0088 decision 8). Read-only attributes and what SCIM does not own are left.

5. **No groups.** `/Groups` is not served. Tinfirm's groups (ADR-0049) are the company's own unit of permission, made and filled by its Owners and Admins for agents, credentials and triggers; an IdP's groups are org charts and mailing lists. Mapping one onto the other would let the IdP grant credentials and agents, and its group names would have to match. The role, the one thing an IdP should decide, is a user attribute (decision 4), which Entra ID fills from an app role and Okta from a profile attribute, both without group push. Provisioning IdP groups into Tinfirm's groups is not done (decided 2026-10-23).

6. **SCIM and sign-in agree on who is who.**
   - A user SCIM creates has an email, a name and a membership with the IdP's `externalId`, but no sign-in yet (`authSource` `SCIM`, no password, no identity).
   - **At their first sign-in** through a SAML or OIDC provider bound to the same company (ADR-0075, ADR-0086), an unknown `(issuer, subject)` is bound to the member of that company whose `scim_external_id` equals the sign-in's subject: SAML's subject (ADR-0086 decision 5), OIDC's `sub`, or for an Entra ID issuer the ID token's `oid`, which the server reads already. The user must be one SCIM created (`authSource` `SCIM`), with no identity and no password, active, and **not an OWNER** of any company: an identity is never bound to an owner's account, so that a SCIM token cannot point an owner's `externalId` at a subject its holder controls (decision 7). A SCIM user made OWNER in the app before their first sign-in gets a sign-in from an instance admin (a password, CONVENTIONS §5) or signs in before the role is given. The binding is audited (`user.identity_bound`, with the provider) and the user's `authSource` becomes `SAML` or `OIDC`. From then on the user is identified by that `(issuer, subject)`, as any other.
   - **By email, only when the provider says so.** A provider's `_SCIM_MATCH` (`TINFIRM_AUTH_SAML_PROVIDERS_<ID>_SCIM_MATCH`, ADR-0086 decision 1, and the same new setting for an OIDC registration, `TINFIRM_AUTH_OIDC_PROVIDERS_<ID>_SCIM_MATCH`) is `EXTERNAL_ID` by default; `EXTERNAL_ID_OR_EMAIL` also binds, when no `externalId` matched, a SCIM-created member of that company without an identity whose email is the one the sign-in asserts under the provider's email trust and domains. This is the one link by email the server makes, limited to a user the same company's IdP created and the same company's provider asserts; the guides use it only when the IdP cannot send the same id in both.
   - **What does not bind** falls through to ADR-0086 decision 7 and CONVENTIONS §5: a new user when the provider auto-provisions, refused otherwise; a new user's email that a SCIM user holds is `EMAIL_IN_USE`, which shows the misaligned ids at once. A company provisioned by SCIM sets its providers' `_AUTO_PROVISION=false`, so that only people the IdP assigned can sign in. A SAML registration has the setting (ADR-0086 decision 1); an OIDC registration gains it too, `TINFIRM_AUTH_OIDC_PROVIDERS_<ID>_AUTO_PROVISION`, whose default is the instance's `TINFIRM_AUTH_OIDC_AUTO_PROVISION`, so that one company's SCIM does not turn off just-in-time users for the other companies of the instance.
   - **The guides align the ids**: Entra ID sends `externalId` from `objectId` (not the default `mailNickname`) and the SAML subject from `objectidentifier`, and its OIDC `oid` is the same id; Okta sends its user id as `externalId` by default, which is its OIDC `sub` and the SAML NameID `user.getInternalProperty("id")`.

7. **Create, update, deactivate, delete.**
   - **Create** (`POST /Users`). A `userName` that a member of the company holds is `409 uniqueness`. Otherwise, a user with that email who is not a member, local or from any provider, becomes a member as when an OWNER adds them by email (CONVENTIONS §6: nothing ties a user to a company); else a new user is created. The membership takes the request's role or `_SCIM_ROLE`, with the seat fallback (decision 8), and the `externalId`; `active: false` creates it suspended. Answer `201` with the user as stored.
   - **Update** (`PUT`, `PATCH`). The role (under `_SCIM_ROLE_SOURCE=IDP`, and only when the request carries it), the `externalId` and `active` are the membership's and apply, but an OWNER's role and `externalId` never change (below). A change of the `externalId` of a member who has no identity yet is audited with both values' hashes, since it decides who may bind to that account (decision 6). The email and name are the user's, the instance's: they change only for a user who is a member of this company alone and whom SCIM created or bound, so that one company's IdP never renames a person another company also has; otherwise they are left and the answer shows them as stored. An email another user holds is `409 uniqueness`.
   - **SCIM's block and an admin's are kept apart.** The membership holds SCIM's suspension on its own (`scim_suspended_at`) beside the principal's suspension by an admin (§15.13), and the user holds SCIM's deactivation on its own (`scim_deactivated_at`) beside an instance admin's (`active=false`). A member is suspended while either holds; a user is inactive while either holds. SCIM sets and clears only its own; an admin's suspension or deactivation, before or after SCIM's, stays until an admin lifts it, whatever SCIM writes.
   - **Deactivate** (`active: false`). Access ends **at once** and the data stays: SCIM's suspension is set, which suspends the member in the company as `PrincipalSwitches.suspendMember` does (CONVENTIONS §15.13), so every company path refuses them, their personal tokens and OAuth grants answer as a non-member's, their running turns are cancelled, their LiteLLM key is blocked and they decide nothing. When they are a member of no other company, or are suspended in every one, SCIM's deactivation of the user is set too, which ends their sessions everywhere (CONVENTIONS §5). Their conversations, agents, triggers, memory and files stay as they were. **Reactivate** (`active: true`) clears SCIM's suspension and SCIM's deactivation, and nothing else: a member an admin also suspended, or a user an instance admin also deactivated, stays blocked, and `active` answers `false`. A seat role given back takes the seat fallback.
   - **Delete** (`DELETE /Users/{id}`). The member leaves the company as a member an admin removes does (`principal.left`, the departure's checklist for the Owners and Admins, §15.13): no access, their content kept, the agents and triggers they own handed over through the checklist. A user who is then a member of no company is deactivated. `204`; the user is `404` to the token after. **Erasure stays a separate act**: SCIM never erases a person's content; the OWNER does, on the retention page (`MANAGE_RETENTION`, CONVENTIONS §15.11), as for anyone.
   - **The OWNER.** SCIM never makes an OWNER, never changes an OWNER's role (a role attribute is ignored for them, and answered as `owner`) and never sets or changes an OWNER's `externalId` (ignored, and answered as stored), and no sign-in binds to an OWNER (decision 6), so that a leaked token can neither take a company's ownership nor redirect an owner's account to a subject its holder controls. It may deactivate or delete an OWNER, so that an owner who left the company loses access, but never **the last active OWNER**: `400 mutability` with the detail `LAST_OWNER`, and the OWNER is told in their inbox. (Decided 2026-10-23.)

8. **Seats: the fallback to VIEWER, never `SEATS_EXHAUSTED`.** Under a key that counts seats, a role that holds one, asked by SCIM for a person without a seat while none is free (a create, a role change, a reactivation), gives **VIEWER** instead: the request succeeds and answers the role given (`viewer`), `member.added` or `member.role.changed` carries `seatFallback: true` and the role asked, and the company's Owners and Admins get the `SEAT_FALLBACK` item (ADR-0077 decision 5, CONVENTIONS §15.24) with the source `scim`. A person who holds a seat keeps it, as ever. Why not `409 SEATS_EXHAUSTED`, as an admin gets in the app:
   - SCIM is a sync loop, not a person at a form. A refused create leaves the person with no user, so they cannot even sign in and read; ADR-0077 decided that signing in never fails for want of a seat, and SCIM's new users are a provider's new users.
   - IdPs treat errors as failures of the whole connection: Entra ID puts a provisioning job whose operations keep failing into quarantine, which stops the company's deactivations too, the part of SCIM that protects it; Okta leaves each failed task for an admin to retry by hand.
   - The fallback heals itself: the answer shows `viewer`, so Entra ID, which compares Tinfirm's values with its own at each cycle, asks for the role again, and gets it once a seat is free; with Okta, an admin re-pushes the user after freeing a seat. The `SEAT_FALLBACK` item says so.

   SCIM grants the roles that hold a seat, with this fallback (decided 2026-10-23).

9. **Audit.** In the company's chain, by the token as actor (decision 3): `scim.user.created`, `scim.user.changed` (the names of the attributes changed, never their values), `scim.user.deactivated`, `scim.user.reactivated` and `scim.user.deleted`, each with the user, beside the core's own events of the same transaction (`user.created`, `member.added`, `member.role.changed`, `principal.suspended` with cause `SCIM`, `principal.resumed`, `principal.left`, `user.updated`), which carry `seatFallback` and the role asked where it applies. `scim.token.created`, `scim.token.revoked`, `scim.refused` and `user.identity_bound` as decisions 3 and 6 say. A `GET` is not audited. The audit holds no email (CONVENTIONS §8): the user's id, never the `userName`.

10. **The edition, and after the licence ends.** SCIM serves while the licence holds `SCIM`: Enterprise in force, `EXPIRING` and `GRACE`. **After the grace (`EXPIRED`) SCIM stops** (ADR-0077 decision 7): every `/scim/v2` request with a valid token answers `403` with the detail `EDITION_REQUIRED`, and no token can be issued. Nothing is undone: the tokens stay (and can be revoked), the members SCIM made and their roles, suspensions and `externalId`s stay, and sign-in keeps binding SCIM users (ADR-0086 keeps SAML; OIDC is every edition's). The `LICENCE` inbox item says, from 30 days before the end, that SCIM will stop and that a person who leaves must then be removed in the app. A new Enterprise key restores it at once; the IdP's next cycle catches up. Under Community never licensed, or Pro, the same `403`.

11. **The API and the web app.**
    - `api/openapi.yaml` describes `/scim/v2/**` under the tag `Scim`, with `application/scim+json` and SCIM's error schema, so that the contract and the generated interface hold it; it is not company-scoped by path, so a test of its own proves that a token of one company never reads or changes another's members (`ScimTenantIsolationTest`).
    - `GET /api/companies/{companyId}/scim/tokens` (OWNER and ADMIN, an instance admin's ADMIN view; every edition), `POST` and `DELETE …/{tokenId}` (OWNER, decision 3); `TokenView {id, name, createdAt, createdBy, createdByName, expiresAt, lastUsedAt, revokedAt}`, never the token but in `POST`'s answer.
    - ADR-0086's `GET /api/companies/{companyId}/sign-in` carries `scim {enabled, baseUrl, defaultRole}`.
    - The web app's **Settings → Sign-in** shows SCIM's part: whether the deployment allows it (and if not, that the operator sets `companies[].scim.enabled`), the base URL to copy, the default role, the tokens with their last use, a token shown once on creation with a copy button, and the guides for Entra ID and Okta. The OWNER issues and revokes; ADMIN reads.
    - New problem codes: `SCIM_NOT_ENABLED`, `SCIM_TOKEN_LIMIT` (409).

    *As built:* ADR-0086's `GET /api/companies/{companyId}/sign-in` answers the providers only; `GET /api/companies/{companyId}/scim` answers SCIM's part (`ScimStatus {enabled, licensed, baseUrl, defaultRole, roleSource}`). The tokens and the status are under the tag `Provisioning`, so that `/scim/v2/**` alone is `Scim` and answers in SCIM's form.
12. **Where the code lives.** Under `ee/`, in the company module's package (`ee/src/main/java/com/flowtivo/tinfirm/company/internal/scim/`): the directory behind the endpoint, the mapping of SCIM's resources and patches, the token store, issuance and revocation. The core keeps the endpoint's controller and its chain, which answer `403` without `ee/`, and reaches the directory and the tokens through ports of its own (`company.internal.ScimDirectory`, `company.internal.ScimTokens`), found when used. The core also keeps what every edition needs: revoking a token and listing them, the binding at sign-in (decision 6, in the identity module's provisioning that ADR-0086 decision 11 shares between OIDC and SAML), and the suspension's cause `SCIM` in the principal module. A membership's change goes through the company module's own API as the Members API's does, with its checks (`LAST_OWNER`, the seat check's lock `licence:seats`).

    *As built:* the directory is the `ee` module's (`com.flowtivo.tinfirm.ee.scim`), not the company module's package: code in that package counts as the company module, which may not depend on the principal and conversation modules the directory needs (Spring Modulith's cycle check). The company module exposes what the directory needs as its named interface `company.scim`, with the ports `ScimDirectory` and `ScimTokens` there. The binding at sign-in is built for OIDC and, with ADR-0086, for SAML.

13. **Compatibility.**

    | | Entra ID | Okta |
    |---|---|---|
    | Connection | "Tenant URL" `https://<host>/scim/v2?aadOptscim062020` (the flag makes Entra ID's `PATCH` follow RFC 7644), "Secret Token" the `tfs_` token | SCIM 2.0 "Base URL" `https://<host>/scim/v2`, authentication "HTTP Header", the token as Bearer; "Unique identifier field" `userName` |
    | Its test | `GET /Users?filter=userName eq "<random>"`, which must answer `200` with an empty list | `GET /Users?startIndex=1&count=2` |
    | Matching | `GET /Users?filter=userName eq "…"`, then `externalId eq` | `GET /Users?filter=userName eq "…"` |
    | Create | `POST` | `POST`, with `password`, which Tinfirm ignores ("Sync password" off) |
    | Update | `PATCH`, `op` in any case (`Replace`), values sometimes strings (`"False"`) | `PUT` with the whole user; `PATCH` for `active` |
    | Deactivate | `PATCH active=false`; `DELETE` when the user is deleted for good, 30 days later | `PATCH active=false`; never `DELETE` |
    | Role | an app role per role (`Tinfirm.Admin`, `.Member`, `.Viewer`); the mapping `SingleAppRoleAssignment([appRoleAssignments])` to the extension's `role`, its values mapped to `admin`, `member`, `viewer` | a profile attribute of the app, `role`, external namespace `urn:ietf:params:scim:schemas:extension:tinfirm:2.0:User`, set per group assignment |
    | `externalId` | change the mapping from `mailNickname` to `objectId` | its user id, by default |
    | Groups | provision users only (assigning a group to the app provisions its members); group objects off | "Push Groups" off |
    | Rate | a cycle every 40 minutes; honours `429` | honours `429` and `Retry-After` |
    | Reach | Microsoft's provisioning service calls from Azure's addresses: the instance's `/scim/v2` must be reachable from the internet, or the customer uses Entra ID's on-premises provisioning agent | from Okta's cloud, or Okta's on-premises provisioning agent |

    ADFS has no SCIM client; an ADFS customer provisions with Entra ID Connect's sync to Entra ID and Entra ID's SCIM, or not at all. Other SCIM clients (OneLogin, JumpCloud, Keycloak's community SCIM extension) are expected to work with the same endpoint, untested.

14. **Testing.**
    - **The endpoint** (`ScimIntegrationTest`): each operation and filter; `ServiceProviderConfig`, `ResourceTypes` and `Schemas` validated against RFC 7643's schemas; every refusal (`invalidFilter`, `uniqueness`, `mutability` for the last OWNER, `invalidValue` for `owner`); the token's lifecycle (issued once, hashed, at most two, revoked, expired, a disabled company's); deactivation ending access at once (a session, a personal token and a running turn of the member) and keeping the content; delete as a member who leaves; the seat fallback on create, role change and reactivation, with the `SEAT_FALLBACK` item; another company's token (`ScimTenantIsolationTest`).
    - **Recorded traffic of the IdPs** (`ScimEntraIdCompatibilityTest`, `ScimOktaCompatibilityTest`): the requests that Entra ID (with and without `aadOptscim062020`) and Okta send in their documented sequences (the connection test, matching, create, update, deactivate, reactivate, delete), kept as fixtures and replayed, with the answers they expect.
    - **SCIM and SAML together, against Keycloak in CI** (planned; `SamlProvisioningTest` covers the binding at a SAML sign-in without Keycloak): a user created through SCIM with an `externalId`, then signed in through the Keycloak realm of ADR-0086 decision 16 whose NameID is that id, is bound to the SCIM user; with another id, refused under `_AUTO_PROVISION=false`; with `EXTERNAL_ID_OR_EMAIL`, bound by email.
    - **The licence**: `EditionGatesIntegrationTest` gains SCIM refused under Community and Pro and after an Enterprise key expired, served in `GRACE`, and revoking a token in every edition.
    - **Before the first release with SCIM**: Microsoft's SCIM Validator and Okta's SCIM 2.0 test suite run against a public test instance, and the guides are followed against real tenants; `docs-site/` records the date each was checked.

## Consequences

- An Enterprise customer's IdP decides who is a member, with which role, and removes a leaver at once, from the same place as their other applications. Threat model 3.6 and R8 (no logout from the IdP) are covered for a company that uses SCIM.
- A leaver's content is never lost through SCIM; erasing it stays the OWNER's act. A company that deletes people at the IdP still holds their conversations until it erases them.
- An Owner can be locked out by a leaked SCIM token only down to the last active OWNER; an instance admin's break-glass appoints an owner again (CONVENTIONS §6).
- An instance that is not reachable from the internet needs the IdP's on-premises agent for SCIM; air-gapped instances get no SCIM from a cloud IdP.
- The role follows the IdP at once with SCIM, and at the next sign-in without it ([ADR-0088](0088-roles-from-the-identity-provider.md)); when SCIM manages a membership, its role wins over a sign-in's claim.
- Under Pro's seat logic the IdP can believe someone is a Member while Tinfirm made them a Viewer; the `SEAT_FALLBACK` item and the answer's role show it, and Entra ID heals it.
- After the licence ends, the IdP's provisioning fails with `403` and leavers stay until an admin removes them; the `LICENCE` notice warns ahead.
- The threat model gains TB17 (an IdP's SCIM client and the server) and the asset A40 (the SCIM tokens).
- CONVENTIONS changes when it is built: §4 (`TINFIRM_COMPANIES_<KEY>_SCIM_ENABLED`, `_SCIM_ROLE`, `tinfirm.scim.*`, and an OIDC registration's `_AUTO_PROVISION` and `_SCIM_MATCH`), §7 (SCIM's bounded offset, the one exception to keyset pagination), §5 (the SCIM token beside the bearer credentials; the binding of a SCIM user at sign-in, the one link by email), §6 (the rows for the tokens: read by OWNER and ADMIN, issued and revoked by OWNER, `MANAGE_PROVISIONING`; the codes), §8 (the actions of decision 9, the actor `SCIM`), §15.13 (the suspension's cause `SCIM`), §15.24 (the core ports and `ee/`'s implementation in the table of paid features' code, the `SCIM` row of the gates, the tests).

## Alternatives considered

- **`SEATS_EXHAUSTED` for SCIM as for an admin.** Decision 8's reasons: it stops the whole sync, quarantines Entra ID's job and leaves a person unable to sign in.
- **The SCIM token in the deployment** (a hash in `TINFIRM_COMPANIES_<KEY>_SCIM_TOKEN_SHA256`). Consistent with ADR-0075, but every rotation is a restart, and the operator must hand a secret to the company's IdP admin. The deployment keeps the yes (decision 2); the token is the OWNER's. Not chosen (decided 2026-10-23).
- **OAuth 2.0 client credentials for SCIM.** Entra ID's gallery apps can, but a non-gallery app and Okta take a bearer token; Tinfirm's authorization server would need a client per company. Not now.
- **A base URL per company** (`/scim/v2/{companyKey}`). The token names the company already; a path adds nothing to the isolation and one more value to copy.
- **Groups provisioned into Tinfirm's groups.** Decision 5's reasons; not done (decided 2026-10-23).
- **Deactivation as removal from the company, or as the user's deactivation alone.** Removal starts a departure the IdP did not ask for and loses the role on a temporary block; the user's deactivation alone would end access to every company for one company's IdP. Suspension in the company, and the user's deactivation when no other company holds them, does what the IdP means.
- **SCIM erasing on `DELETE`.** Entra ID deletes for good 30 days after a user is deleted, and a mistaken unassignment would destroy a company's records. Erasure stays a deliberate act.
- **Linking SCIM users by email by default.** It would break the rule that sign-in never links by email (threat model 3.3), however narrowly; `externalId` is what the IdP itself uses, and email stays an opt-in per provider.

## The owner's decisions on the open points

All decided on 2026-10-23, as proposed; none is open.

1. **Groups**: no `/Groups`; the role is a user attribute (decision 5).
2. **Seat roles**: SCIM grants `admin` and `member`, with the fallback to VIEWER, never `SEATS_EXHAUSTED` (decision 8).
3. **The OWNER**: SCIM may deactivate or delete an OWNER, never the last active one, and never makes or demotes one (decision 7).
4. **The token**: issued in the app by the OWNER, the deployment allowing SCIM per company (decisions 2, 3).
5. **Binding by email**: by `externalId`, and by email only where a provider sets `_SCIM_MATCH=EXTERNAL_ID_OR_EMAIL` (decision 6).
