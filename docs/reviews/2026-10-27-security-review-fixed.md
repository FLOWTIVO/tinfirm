# The security review of 2026-10-27: the fix round

Status: the 76 findings of [the security review of 2026-10-27](2026-10-27-security-review.md) (H1 to H4, M1 to M18, L1 to L35, I1 to I10, D1 to D9) fixed, accepted, left open or withdrawn as [the plan](2026-10-27-security-review-plan.md) and the owner's decisions Q1 to Q8 say, in packages WP1 to WP14 · Owner: J. Balcar

Each package read the findings it closes against the code, fixed them in its own area with unit tests that fail before the fix where the finding allowed one and integration cases where the proof needs PostgreSQL or Docker, and updated the threat model's rows in the same change. WP13 took the follow-ups the other packages found; WP12 wrote the documents (the threat model's positions, lifetimes and reveal, the ADRs, this record). `docs/CONVENTIONS.md` and `docs/PLATFORM.md` were not edited (CONTRIBUTING §8); §3 holds the proposed wording.
