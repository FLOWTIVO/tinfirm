# Tinfirm documentation

Tinfirm is a self-hosted platform on which a company runs AI agents, each with a role, instructions, tools and access. People talk to them in Tinfirm, from their own AI client, or in Slack and Microsoft Teams. Triggers start them from the systems the company already uses: Jira, GitHub, GitLab, a chat channel, a schedule or a webhook. An agent does a bounded job in a sandbox, with the accounts its audience allows, and brings every decision that needs a person to one inbox.

Choose the guide for the work you do.
